02 — The language of life

Every living thing has a story written in DNA.

DNA is a double helix: two long strands twisted around each other.

Look closer. The two strands are joined by pairs of bases.

Unzip it, and each base fits only one partner. A pairs with T. G pairs with C.

The order of the bases is a code. It carries the instructions for building and running every living thing.

  • AAdenine
  • TThymine
  • GGuanine
  • CCytosine

A always pairs with T, and G always pairs with C.
