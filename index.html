Before you join

Good questions. Clear answers.

Which subjects and levels do you teach?

Form 1 to Form 3 Science, Form 4 and Form 5 Science for SPM, and SPM Biology.

How much are the fees?

Fees depend on the level and the type of class. Send a WhatsApp message and Ms Rubini will share the current fees for the class you want.

Where are the classes held?

Physical classes run in Klang and Petaling Jaya. The exact venue for your class is shared when you enquire.

Can I join online?

Yes. There are online group classes and online one-to-one sessions, so students outside Klang, PJ and KL can join too.

What is the timetable?

Class times differ by level and location. Message Ms Rubini on WhatsApp for the latest slots.

How do I sign up?

Tap any WhatsApp button on this page. Tell Ms Rubini the student's form and subject, and she will take it from there.

Ready when you are.

Questions about classes, fees or the next seminar? Ms Rubini would love to hear from you.
